---
title: Costs
---
To capture the complete financial picture of moving a product from a factory floor to a customer's hands, you must look at expenses across four distinct stages: Manufacturing, Landed Cost, Holding/Fulfillment, and Post-Sale.

| Stage                                           | Cost Category        | Specific Expense Items                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Manufacturing  <br>_(Before Shipping)_       | Production Cost      | Raw materials, factory labor, tooling/molds, and manufacturer markups.      |
|                                                 | Quality Control      | Third-party inspection fees, laboratory testing, and sample creation.       |
| 2. Landed Cost  <br>_(During Transit)_          | Product Price        | The actual wholesale or FOB (Free on Board) price paid to the supplier.     |
|                                                 | Freight & Shipping   | Ocean/air freight, fuel surcharges, port fees, and final-mile delivery.     |
|                                                 | Duties & Taxes       | Country-specific import tariffs, custom agent fees, and local excise taxes. |
|                                                 | Cargo Insurance      | Premium paid to protect the shipment against loss, theft, or damage at sea. |
|                                                 | Handling & Brokerage | Customs broker fees, documentation/bill of lading fees, and palletization.  |
| 3. Holding & Fulfillment  <br>_(After Arrival)_ | Storage Costs        | Warehouse rent, pallet storage fees, climate control, and security.         |
|                                                 | Pick and Pack        | Fulfillment center labor to pick individual items and pack boxes.           |
|                                                 | Packaging Materials  | Branded boxes, bubble wrap, tape, inserts, and shipping labels.             |
|                                                 | Inventory Carrying   | Opportunity cost of tied-up capital and interest on inventory financing.    |
| 4. Post-Sale  <br>_(After the Sale)_            | Customer Shipping    | Outbound shipping fees to send the product to the end consumer.             |
|                                                 | Returns & Exchanges  | Reverse logistics shipping, restocking labor, and unsellable damaged goods. |
|                                                 | Shrinkage & Waste    | Inventory lost to theft, expiration, obsolescence, or warehouse damage.     |
