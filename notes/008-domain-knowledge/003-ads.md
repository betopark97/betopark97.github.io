## Google Ads

rate metrics:

- engagement_rate = engagements / impressions
- video_view_rate = video_views / impressions
- average_cpv = cost / video_views
- cost_per_all_conversions = cost / all_conversions
- value_per_all_conversions = all_conversions_value / all_conversions
