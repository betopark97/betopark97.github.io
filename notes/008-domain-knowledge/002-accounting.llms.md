# Accounting

## Types of Costs

### Product Costs (Inventoriable Costs)

These are the costs directly tied to acquiring or producing inventory. After they are sold, the become Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).\
\#### Landed Costs

- freight/shipping, import duties, custom fees, insurance during transit, and handling fees.
- these are used for the unit cost

#### Direct Materials & Direct Labor

- raw materials and hands-on manufacturing labor needed to make a product.

### Period Costs

- warehouse rent, electricity, storage equipment, staff salaries, etc.
- these are operating expenses and are recorded on the profit and loss statement at the end of the month rather than in the unit cost.
